About JNPDI

JNPDI is an evidence-based research initiative exploring how judicial performance, equitable access to justice, predictable economic dispute resolution, digital transformation, and institutional integrity support trust and long-term national development in Indonesia.

All public artifacts enforce strict data hygiene: zero imputation for missing values, zero personal identifiers (PII), mandatory source provenance, and automated blocking of restricted (P3/P4) records.

Five Research Domains

DOM-01 · 10 Indicators

Judicial Performance

Measures case clearance rates, average disposition time, backlog ratios, appeal/cassation consistency, mediation success, and judgment execution.

DOM-02 · 10 Indicators

Access to Justice

Tracks prodeo fee-waiver coverage, Posbakum legal aid posts, mobile courts (Sidang Keliling), disability accessibility, and small-claims adoption.

DOM-03 · 10 Indicators

Business & Economic Justice

Monitors commercial contract resolution, bankruptcy/PKPU clearance, IP disputes, industrial relations, tax court clearance, and MSME recovery.

DOM-04 · 10 Indicators

Digital Judiciary

Evaluates e-Court filing penetration, e-Litigation adoption, SIPP sync compliance, Direktori Putusan publication rates, and upload latency.

DOM-05 · 10 Indicators

Institutional Integrity

Assesses LHKPN wealth disclosure compliance, WBK/WBBM integrity zones, SIWAS complaint resolution, BPK audit opinions, and public satisfaction.

Research & Publications

Five reproducible Jupyter notebooks accompany the datasets, separating descriptive synthetic demonstrations from causal claims:

  • 01_judicial_performance_trends.ipynb — Case clearance, disposition time, and workload trends.
  • 02_access_to_justice_distribution.ipynb — Fee-waiver, Posbakum, and geographic accessibility patterns.
  • 03_business_economic_disputes.ipynb — Commercial, PKPU, IP, and MSME dispute resolution metrics.
  • 04_digital_judiciary_adoption.ipynb — e-Court, e-Litigation, and Direktori Putusan adoption trajectories.
  • 05_integrated_justice_prosperity.ipynb — Cross-domain synthesis linking judicial capacity to prosperity frameworks.

Methodology, Provenance & Data Governance

12-Point Automated Validation

Every CSV passes checks for required columns, unique record IDs, valid indicator IDs, domain alignment, finite numeric values, source provenance, duplicate observations, classification bounds, approval status, synthetic tagging, and unit/range consistency.

Fail-Closed Publication Gate

Data is classified from P1 (Public Official Approved) to P5 (Synthetic Demonstration). Restricted P3 and confidential P4 records are automatically blocked by CI/CD before any public release.
